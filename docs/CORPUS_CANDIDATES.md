# Candidate public corpus — infection prevention and control

Scope: staff reference questions about infection prevention and control in healthcare settings. The 20 PDFs below were downloaded from CDC sources on 22 November 2026 and checked for PDF signatures, SHA-256 hashes and readable page text. They remain **candidates**, not a supervisor-approved corpus. Raw PDFs stay in ignored `data/raw/`; exact URLs, snapshot dates and hashes are in `data/candidate-manifest.jsonl`.

The [CDC guideline library](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/guidance/index.html) is the source directory. Before approval, check exact editions, superseded guidance, content quality, permitted use and whether the source scope matches the course brief. Do not treat a downloadable public guideline as course approval.
